# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this community:

- Being kind and patient with questions, especially from newcomers.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Crediting others' work and respecting differing viewpoints.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments; sexualised language or imagery.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing another person's browsing data, URLs or screenshots of their setup.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not
comply, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour
they deem inappropriate.

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour by opening a
report through GitHub (see the repository's **Security** tab, or the
maintainer's profile). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.
